Write the letter of the correct meaning next to each term. One meaning is a distractor.

TermLetterMeanings
1 optimiseA Improve a solution against a goal, usually by trading off another dimension
2 performance evidenceB A measured result that proves a claim, such as a before-and-after number
3 verify-before-trustC Check an output against the task and a trusted source before believing it
4 feed-forwardD Feedback that names a specific next step, not just praise
5 calibrationE Reviewers agreeing on a standard, settled by evidence
F A goal with no target
